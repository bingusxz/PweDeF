# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

(Electron desktop app for Windows; UI is HTML/CSS/React rendered in Chromium.)

## Users

Mandaue Foam office staff (admin, sales, accounting, HR) working all day on office Windows PCs. They handle invoices, purchase orders, delivery receipts, contracts and HR forms: editing, signing, filling, merging, converting and redacting PDFs, often many times an hour.

## Product Purpose

An all-in-one, in-house PDF studio that replaces paid Adobe Acrobat Pro seats and web tools like iLovePDF. Success means staff can do any PDF job without leaving the app or paying per seat, and the owner can ship updates that reach everyone automatically.

## Positioning

Acrobat Pro's editing and signing depth combined with iLovePDF's breadth of one-click conversions in a single desktop app. It's free per seat, works offline with local files, and its Office conversion uses the Microsoft Office already installed on the PC.

## Operating Context

- Long sessions on office desktops and laptops (roughly 1366–1920 px wide), mouse and keyboard, sometimes a pen or touchpad for signatures.
- Files come from email attachments, scanners and shared folders; outputs go back to email or folders.
- Distributed by the owner sending the installer; auto-updates from GitHub Releases on every push to main.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Edit existing text, add text, images and shapes, whiteout, true redaction, highlight, comments, sign (draw, type or upload) and stamps, fill forms.
- Organize pages, merge, split, watermark, Bates numbering, OCR, batch processing.
- Convert Word, Excel, PowerPoint, images, web pages and HTML to PDF; convert PDF to Word, Excel, PowerPoint, images and text.
- Tools must always be one click away; findability is non-negotiable.
- Must offer both light and dark appearance (follow the system by default, with a manual toggle).

## Brand Commitments

- Name: **Acrobatics**. It's a standalone brand, not Mandaue Foam branded, and must not resemble Adobe Acrobat.
- The owner wants it to feel unique. The feature set is what's fixed; the current visual design is explicitly not.

## Evidence on Hand

No testimonials, metrics or customer claims exist. Don't fabricate any.

## Product Principles

1. Every tool is one click from where you are.
2. The document is the hero; chrome stays out of its way during long sessions.
3. Honest about capability: say what a tool really does (for example, cover vs. true redaction).
4. Owned, not rented: no accounts, no upsells, works offline.
